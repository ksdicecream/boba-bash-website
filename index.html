Snow Leopard

Panthera uncia, also known as the snow leopard is one of the world's most elusive creatures, located in the mountain ranges of Asia. They are sometimes known as the "ghost of the mountains" due to their elusive nature.

Snow leopards are currently listed as Vulnerable under the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species.
